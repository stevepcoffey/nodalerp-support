The operational core between your CRM and your financials.

01 — The Flow

From order to ship — one connected system.

A sales order from your CRM lands in Nodal in seconds. From there, every downstream action — planning, purchasing, production, shipping — flows automatically through the same operational fabric.

Order intake

Sales orders arrive from HubSpot or any CRM via API. Validated against the item master and pushed to the production queue automatically. No rekeying.

Inventory

Real-time visibility across zones, aisles, and bins. Lot and serial tracking, cycle counts, audit trails — all logged automatically with reason codes.

MRP & planning

Material requirements planning runs across open demand, current stock, and open POs. Suggests work orders and purchases — with a frozen-zone time fence to prevent schedule chaos.

Purchasing

Demand-driven procurement. Purchase requisitions flow from MRP to the buyer queue. Supplier performance tracked. Receiving updates inventory instantly.

Production scheduling

Auto-schedule by due date or earliest slot, respecting work center calendars. Drag-and-drop adjustments. Late-job, material, and capacity alerts in real time.

KPI & analytics

Role-based dashboards. Schedule attainment, throughput, variance, scrap rates, bottleneck queues, inventory turns — live as the data posts.

02 — The Boundary

We don't replace your CRM. We don't replace your financials.

Nodal connects to both — via API, in real time — and runs everything in between with a depth and discipline that neither system is designed to handle on its own.

CRM stays your CRM

HubSpot, Salesforce, or whatever you use. Deals close there. Nodal receives them as sales orders via inbound API — instantly, without manual handoff.

Nodal runs the middle

Order intake → inventory → MRP → purchasing → production → KPIs. The operational engine where manufacturing is actually won or lost.

Financials stay your financials

QuickBooks today. Something else later. Nodal pushes clean, structured transaction data via API — inventory events, COGS, purchase data, production variances.

03 — Built In

What you'd otherwise pay extra for.

Most ERPs charge for these as separate modules or premium tiers. In Nodal, they're part of the platform.

Multi-warehouse

Zones, aisles, bins. Multi-location stock tracking with transfer workflows.

Lot & serial tracking

Full traceability from raw material to finished product. Recall-ready.

Quality & scrap

Inspection checks tied to work orders. Scrap auto-logged with reason codes.

Cost rollup

BOM-driven cost calculation with variance analysis on labor and material.

Capacity planning

Work center load vs. available hours. Overload flagged before release.

Audit logs

Every transaction stamped with user, timestamp, and reason code.

Mobile access

Tablet floor app, mobile warehouse app. Built for where the work happens.

AI-ready data

Data fabric structured for ML from day one. No data warehouse required.
