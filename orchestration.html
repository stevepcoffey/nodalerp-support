Integration that understands what it's connecting.

01 — The Difference

Pipes vs. business logic.

MuleSoft, Workato, and Zapier are sophisticated pipes — they move data between systems based on field mappings. Nodal's orchestration layer is different: it encodes real business rules natively, so connecting a system doesn't mean training generic middleware to understand what an order is.

Event-driven, not batch

Every transaction emits a structured event the moment it happens. Dashboards, analytics, connected systems, and AI consumers all react in real time. No overnight syncs.

Business-aware orchestration

Credit checks, inventory availability, make-vs-buy logic, work center capacity — encoded in the layer itself. Adding a connector means connecting to a system that already understands manufacturing.

Resilient by design

If a downstream API goes offline — freight, financials, HR — Nodal queues the action, preserves state, and retries when connectivity returns. No lost transactions. No operator clean-up.

Documented end to end

Every object in the system — orders, work orders, inventory, vendors, employees — has a stable, documented REST API surface. Not the subset the vendor chose to expose.

02 — What Connects

The systems you already use.

Nodal's orchestration layer connects to your CRM on one side and your financial system on the other — plus the operational tools in between. Through the same framework, not bespoke integrations.

Inbound

CRM

HubSpot, Salesforce. Confirmed deals push to Nodal as sales orders in real time. Customer info, line items, dates — no manual entry.

Outbound

Financials

QuickBooks Online today. Receipts, COGS, purchase transactions, and production variances push as they happen — clean, structured, no double entry.

Bidirectional

HR & Payroll

UKG and similar. Labor time captured at work orders flows to payroll. Employee data flows back for badge scan login on the floor.

Outbound

Freight

Freightwise and similar. Shipment data triggers carrier rating. Delivery confirmations close out the order automatically.

Outbound

Tax

Avalara. Real-time tax calculation against sales orders and purchases. Compliance handled without a separate workflow.

Custom

Your stack

EDI partners, custom apps, legacy systems. The same connector framework handles them — no bespoke project per integration.
