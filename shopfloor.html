01 — The Operator Workflow

From badge to finished good — five taps.

A floor operator's workflow shouldn't feel like an ERP. It should feel like a tool. Here's what an operator actually does, station-side, in the course of a job.

01

Badge in

Scan badge at the tablet. No passwords. Logged in with role-based permissions.

02

Pick a work order

See assigned jobs. Tap to open. Routing and BOM are right there.

03

Clock into operation

One tap to start. Labor time captured automatically against the work order.

04

Scan materials

Scan as you consume. Real-time inventory deduction. Full lot traceability.

05

Report & complete

Log quality, scrap, or completion. Next operation gets the handoff automatically.

02 — Built for the Floor

Designed by people who've stood at the station.

Generic business software wasn't built for a noisy, dimly-lit, gloved-hand environment. Every screen on the Nodal shop floor app was — because we've watched the people who'd use it actually use it.

Badge scan login

No passwords to forget or share. Badge scan logs in with role-based permissions and personal time tracking.

Big buttons

Designed for gloved hands and quick taps. No tiny menus, no buried screens, no need to slow down to navigate.

Dark mode

Built for overhead lighting and screen glare. High contrast. Easy on the eyes through a full shift.

Scan-driven everything

Materials, work orders, locations, lots. Barcode scanning drives every workflow — no typing, no lookups, no errors.

10-second scrap logging

Failed quality check? Tap a reason code, enter quantity, done. Scrap captured immediately. Quality trends visible the same minute.

Real-time, everywhere

When an operator completes a step, the work order status updates for everyone. No batch processing. No end-of-shift catch-up.

03 — Why It Matters

Accurate data starts where the work happens.

When operators log production at their station — not on a clipboard that gets entered at the end of shift — you get something your current system probably can't deliver: data that's current, accurate, and trustworthy enough to make decisions on.

Operators stay productive

No leaving the station to find a computer. No paperwork piling up to enter later. Less time on overhead, more time on the job.

Leadership sees the truth

Live dashboards reflect what's actually happening on the floor — not a version of reality from yesterday's data entry batch.

Quality issues surface immediately

Scrap and quality fails feed trend reporting in real time. Spot recurring problems in days, not at month-end review.

04 — Deployment

Live on one line in weeks.

The shop floor app can deploy standalone — pilot one production line, prove it works, then expand. No need to roll out the whole ERP first.

Week 1

Scope a single line. Configure stages, work centers, user roles, and badge scans.

Week 2

Load products, materials, and work orders. Train supervisors and operators.

Week 3

Run the pilot. Watch where people get stuck. Adjust screens and workflows.

Week 4

Go live. Then add lines, integrations, and modules as the team's ready.
